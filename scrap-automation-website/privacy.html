Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Scrap Automation may process when it is used through Discord and connected Roblox services.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how the service is used, Scrap Automation may process Discord user IDs, usernames and display information, server and channel information, and Discord roles needed to determine whether a user is authorized to use management features.

The service may also process Roblox usernames and management information supplied through supported commands, such as an amount of in-game money or a custom RP name.

2. Management Logs

Management actions may be logged for administration, auditing, troubleshooting and abuse prevention.

Logs can include the executor, target Roblox username, action performed, relevant value or details, whether the action succeeded or failed, and a timestamp.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To provide and operate Scrap Automation's management features.
  • To verify authorization for restricted management commands.
  • To send supported management requests to the connected Roblox experience.
  • To maintain administrative logs and investigate errors or misuse.
  • To protect the service from unauthorized or abusive use.

4. Third-Party Services

Scrap Automation relies on third-party services including Discord and Roblox services, including Roblox Open Cloud where applicable.

Their own terms and privacy policies may apply to information processed by those platforms.

5. Selling or Advertising

Scrap Automation does not sell personal information to advertisers and is not designed to use collected information for targeted advertising.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain administrative records, troubleshoot issues, prevent abuse, or meet other legitimate operational requirements.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to limit unauthorized access to information handled by the service. However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Requests

If you have a question about information processed by Scrap Automation or would like to request appropriate access, correction or deletion, contact the service administrator through the relevant Discord server or support channel.

9. Children's Privacy

Scrap Automation is not intentionally designed to collect information from children for purposes unrelated to its operation.

Discord and Roblox users are also subject to the age requirements and policies of those platforms.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the Scrap Automation service administrator through the associated Discord community.
